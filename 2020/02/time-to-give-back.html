Hi, I'm Anmol. I go by nhooyr on the internet.
I'm a Canadian software engineer from Toronto, Ontario.
(Nov 11, 2026): There is more up-to-date information on my identity at Rebirth.

I'm a self-taught engineer. I didn't attend university and I didn't graduate high school.

In July 2017, at the end of my junior year of high school I became employed as a full-time engineer at Coder. As I was able to attain gainful employment while I was 17, I felt there was no need for me to continue schooling and so I dropped out during my first month of senior year.

I believe there's little valuable information you cannot learn by yourself because it all comes down to some sequence of NANDs. It's how I check to see whether I've understood something. I break it down into its most fundamental statements and try to realize its axioms.

To be clear, I don't think self-education is a panacea. There are tradeoffs. I've certainly suffered from the negative consequences. I think I have benefited and will continue to benefit overall. People often tell me I'm taking a shortcut but I'm looking 30 years out, not 5 or 10.

The greatest factor in my success as an engineer has been my own passion, initiative and focus. However, the incredible volume of quality reading available on websites, blogs and forums has contributed immensely. For at least the last 8 years, the internet has been my primary means of education.

I wish to contribute back.

On this blog you can expect to find quality writings about software, philosophy, life and everything in between.

I'll be taking my time though. I have no intention of adding more noise to the world.
